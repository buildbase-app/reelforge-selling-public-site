ReelForge is a studio for making short vertical videos (Reels, Shorts, TikToks) that you run on your own computer or server. Your AI agent drives it over REST and MCP: the agent plans every scene and its timing, and ReelForge renders the scenes with four engines (Remotion, Motion Canvas, HTML and HyperFrames) and assembles them into one MP4.

## How it is licensed

- **Source-available.** The code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/buildbase-app/reelforge-studio), so you can read every line of it. It is licensed, not given away: see the [licences](/licence).
- **Free for personal use.** Hobby, study, research and evaluation use is free under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, with a free Personal licence key claimed with a BuildBase account.
- **A one-time Commercial licence for anything that earns money.** Monetised channels, client work, companies. Bought once, not as a subscription. Prices are read live from the store on the [pricing page](/pricing).
- **Every released Docker image needs a licence key from BuildBase.** You run it yourself, on your own machine or cloud server: there is no render service run by us, and the AI agent you connect runs under your own account.
- **No resale, redistribution, rebranding or competing hosted service**, under either licence, and forks must be renamed.

## What is ready today

This store is open: you can read about ReelForge, [join the waitlist](/waitlist), create an account and read the draft legal pages. ReelForge itself is in early access and **not on sale yet**.

## Licensing you should know about

ReelForge includes Remotion-based templates. Remotion is free for individuals and companies of up to 3 people; larger companies need their own Remotion licence ([remotion.dev/license](https://www.remotion.dev/license)). ReelForge also includes Motion Canvas, HTML and HyperFrames engines.
